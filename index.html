Long ago, the old world ran on machines — lights that never went out, voices that carried across the sky. Then it all stopped, and four realms grew over the ruins: the Meadowlands, Thornwood, Dustfall, and Whitepeak. But the old stories speak of a fifth realm, sealed away, where the last of the old world's light still burns. You found an ember that should have died a thousand years ago. Its light woke — and it shows the way. Gather the four realm keys, forge the Master Key, and face the Shadow Knight who guards the light.

How to Play

Move — WASD or arrow keys · left stick on touch

Attack — Space or J · ATTACK button (you start with no weapon — earn one first)

Talk / Use — E · TALK button. Walk up to a villager, a chest, the shrine, or the General Store.

Pause — Esc or P  ·  Bag — I  ·  Map — M

Walk off the edge of a screen to enter the next one. Your embers are your health — a weak hit takes half an ember. Rest at the Meadow Shrine (far south) to heal and save. The game also saves when you enter a new screen.

Goblins dissolve into embers and drop coins. Sunleaf herbs grow in the North Meadow and the Herb Field.
